# ParcelPe refund & claims policy (fictional, for the demo)

## 1. Delayed parcels
- If a parcel is **more than 5 days late**, the customer gets the **shipping fee back**.
- 5 days late or less: no refund. Apologise and share the new ETA.

## 2. Lost parcels
- A parcel is "lost" when tracking has not moved for 7+ days.
- The customer gets the **declared value**, capped at **₹10,000**.

## 3. Damaged parcels
- The claim must be raised **within 48 hours of delivery**.
- The customer must **attach a photo** of the damage.
- Compensation = **declared value**, capped at **₹10,000**.
- Customers with **2 or more claims in the past** are flagged for manual review.

## 4. Refunds when the customer changes their mind
- ParcelPe is a courier, not a seller. We cannot refund the product. Ask the customer to contact the seller.

## 5. Who approves what (the action limit)
- Any payout **above ₹2,000** must be approved by a human support lead.
- Any **flagged** claim must be approved by a human support lead.

## 6. Address changes
- Allowed only while the parcel is **in transit** or **delayed**.
- Not allowed once it is **out for delivery** or **delivered**.
